❓ Shipping FAQs

When will my order be shipped?

Orders placed before 2 PM IST are shipped the same day (Mon–Sat). Orders after 2 PM or on Sunday are shipped the next business day.

Do you deliver on Sundays?

Most courier partners don't deliver on Sundays. However, in metro cities, some deliveries may happen on Sundays depending on the courier.

Can I change my delivery address after ordering?

Yes, if the order hasn't been shipped yet. Contact us at hello@nadanachar.com or WhatsApp +91 98765 43210 immediately.

What if I'm not home during delivery?

The courier will attempt delivery 2-3 times on different days. If all attempts fail, the package is returned to us. Re-shipping charges (₹60) apply in such cases.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes! We ship to 22 countries including UAE, USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Singapore, and more. International shipping charges are calculated at checkout based on weight and destination.

My package arrived damaged. What do I do?

We're so sorry! Email us a photo of the damaged package and jar within 48 hours of delivery at hello@nadanachar.com. We'll send a free replacement immediately.

Can I pick up my order from your Thrissur store?

Yes, you can! Select "Store Pickup" at checkout. Your order will be ready within 4 hours. Address: MG Road, Thrissur, Kerala 680001.
