Our Story

Three generations. One recipe. Infinite love.

🏺

It Started With a Clay Pot in Thrissur

In 1972, Sarojini Amma began pickling mangoes from her backyard in a small village near Thrissur, Kerala. She had no business plan — just a recipe passed down from her mother, a clay pot, and the fierce Kerala sun.

Neighbours noticed. Then their relatives. Then strangers from nearby towns. Word spread that Sarojini Amma's Nadan Achar tasted like no other — because every jar was made by hand, with sun-dried ingredients and cold-pressed sesame oil from Palakkad.

Fifty years later, her grandchildren run the same kitchen. We've scaled up, but we've changed nothing about the process. Same recipes. Same clay pots (bharani). Same patience. Same love.

Today: 50,000+ Families Across the World

From Kochi to Canada, from Chennai to Chicago — our pickles travel across oceans because Keralites carry their taste buds wherever they go. We ship to every pincode in India, and to 22 countries worldwide.
